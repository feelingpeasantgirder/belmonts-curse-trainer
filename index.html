Features

Every cheat the hunt demands

Six toggles built for speedrunners, completionists, and anyone who just wants to survive the clock tower.

🛡️

Infinite HP

Lock your health bar at full so spikes, pits, and Death himself can’t end the run. Toggle on or off at any moment.

🗡️

Unlimited Sub-Weapons

Throw axes, daggers, and holy water without burning hearts. Every sub-weapon stays ready for the next boss door.

🪙

Max Gold

Instantly fill your wallet to the cap so you can buy the whip upgrades and relics you need without grinding candles.

💀

One-Hit Kill Toggle

Turn every enemy into a single-strike kill. Perfect for farming drops or blasting through a fresh playthrough on a whim.

💾

Save-State Hotkeys

Tap a hotkey to snapshot progress and another to restore it instantly. Practice a boss pattern without replaying the whole stage.

⚡

Lightweight & Safe

A single portable executable with no installer, no background service, and no telemetry. Open source and auditable.
